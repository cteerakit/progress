Privacy Policy

Progress for Google Slides · Last updated September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Progress for Google Slides ("the Extension") handles information when you use our Chrome extension.

Summary

The Extension adds slide status labels inside Google Slides. By default, status data stays in your browser. If you choose to sign in with Google, status labels are stored on each slide as hidden marker shapes in the presentation so collaborators can see the same statuses. Status presets and collaborator emails are stored in Google Drive file metadata on that presentation. The Extension does not read slide text, images, or your browsing history, and it does not sell your data.

What Data We Collect

Data stored locally in your browser

  • Slide statuses — Status labels (built-in or custom), internal slide identifiers, update timestamps, and a compact history of prior changes when signed in.
  • Status preset configuration — Customized labels, colors, icons, order, and which statuses count as complete.
  • Signed-in account — Your Google email and profile photo URL after sign-in, shown in the side panel Account view.
  • Presentation identifiers — Google Slides presentation IDs for decks where you have used the Extension.
  • Sync state — Sign-in status, last sync time, and recent sync error messages shown in the side panel.

This data is stored in chrome.storage.local and is used only to power the Extension UI (filmstrip chips, title-bar badge, and side panel).

Data sent to Google when you sign in

  • Authentication — Google OAuth through Chrome's identity API with the presentations, drive.metadata, userinfo.email, and userinfo.profile scopes. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Slide status markers — Status labels stored on each slide as hidden marker shapes with alt-text metadata for presentations you can edit.
  • Status presets and collaborator emails — Stored in Google Drive file metadata on the presentation. Profile photos stay in local cache and are not written to Drive.
  • Presentation access — Marker and Drive metadata read/write only on presentations you open in Google Slides while using the Extension. Slides API requests are limited to slide page IDs and marker alt-text.

Data we do not collect

  • Slide text, speaker notes, images, or other presentation content
  • Browsing history or activity outside Google Slides
  • Analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or advertising identifiers
  • Financial, health, or location information

The Extension does collect email addresses as described above: your signed-in Google email, and collaborator emails stored in Drive file metadata for history attribution.

How We Use Data

We use data solely to provide Extension features: status chips, progress badges, local caching, optional collaborator sync, customizable presets, and the side panel (History, Statuses, and Account). We do not use your data for advertising, creditworthiness, or unrelated purposes.

How Data Is Stored

Data Location When
Slide statuses and presentation cache Your browser While the Extension is installed
Slide status markers Hidden shapes on each slide in the presentation When you are signed in and sync
Status presets and collaborator emails Google Drive file metadata on the presentation When you are signed in and sync
Signed-in email and profile photos Your browser While cached locally
OAuth token Managed by Chrome / Google While you remain signed in

The Extension does not operate its own servers and does not store your data on developer-controlled infrastructure.

Third-Party Services

When you sign in, the Extension uses:

  • Google OAuth / Chrome Identity API
  • Google userinfo API
  • Google Slides API
  • Google Drive API

We do not share your data with any other third parties.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. If you sign in and sync, status markers are stored inside the presentation, and status presets plus collaborator emails are stored in that file's Drive metadata. Collaborators with edit access can read this metadata through Google Slides, Google Drive, and this Extension.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local data — Remains until you clear it or uninstall the Extension. You can reset all slide statuses from the title-bar breakdown panel and clear per-slide history from the side panel.
  • Slide markers — Remain on slides until you reset statuses to No status (which deletes the markers) or remove them manually. Signing out stops further sync.
  • Drive metadata — Presets and collaborator emails remain on the presentation until overwritten by a later sync or removed through Google Drive.
  • Authentication — Click Sign out in the side panel Account view to revoke the cached OAuth token.

Permissions

  • storage — Local cache of slide statuses, presets, and profile photos
  • identity — Optional Google sign-in
  • alarms — Periodic background sync for open presentations
  • sidePanel — History, Statuses, and Account UI; opens when you click the toolbar icon
  • https://docs.google.com/* — Find open Google Slides tabs so chips, presets, and the side panel stay in sync
  • https://slides.googleapis.com/* — Read slide structure and write status marker shapes when signed in
  • https://www.googleapis.com/drive/v3/* — Read and write status preset and collaborator email metadata when signed in
  • https://www.googleapis.com/oauth2/v3/* — Fetch the signed-in user's email and profile photo

Children's Privacy

The Extension is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our data practices change. The date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision. Continued use of the Extension after an update means you accept the revised policy.
